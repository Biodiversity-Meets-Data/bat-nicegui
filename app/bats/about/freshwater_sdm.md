### About this Tool

The Freshwater Habitat Suitability Model Tool predicts habitat suitability for species in European freshwater ecosystems under current and future climate conditions. It combines GBIF species occurrence records with climatic, hydrotopographic, and land-cover variables to develop Random Forest species distribution models. Predictions are generated at the subcatchment level.

### Features

- **Habitat Suitability Maps:** Explore predicted habitat suitability across freshwater subcatchments within a selected study area.

- **Future Climate Projections:** Compare predicted suitability under historical (1981–2010), mid-century (2041–2070), and late-century (2071–2100) climate conditions using the SSP3-7.0 scenario.

- **Habitat Suitability Change:** Visualize spatial changes in predicted suitability between historical and future climate periods.

- **Environmental Variable Importance:** Identify the climatic, hydrotopographic, and land-cover predictors most influential in model predictions.

- **Response Curves:** Explore relationships between predicted habitat suitability and individual environmental variables.

- **Model Evaluation:** Assess predictive performance, including detection of known occurrences, separation from background locations, and potential overfitting.

### Results

The tool generates an interactive dashboard with habitat suitability maps, projected changes under future climate conditions, environmental response curves, and model evaluation indicators.

Higher suitability values indicate environmental conditions associated with species occurrences, rather than confirmed presence or actual invasion probability. Projected changes highlight areas where freshwater habitats may become more or less suitable.

Results should be interpreted considering model performance, occurrence-data limitations, and uncertainty in future environmental conditions.
