# Natura 2000 Habitat Mapping

Combine GBIF occurrence records, EUNIS habitat data, reference species lists,
and the EUNIS-to-Annex I crosswalk for a selected Natura 2000 site.

## Typical Use Cases

- Summarize habitats and species records within a protected site.
- Review Birds and Habitats Directive and invasive-species matches.
- Inspect coordinate quality and occurrence coverage.

Crosswalk matches and occurrence records are not verified conservation-status
assessments.
