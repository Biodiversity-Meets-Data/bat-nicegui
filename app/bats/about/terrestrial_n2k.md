### About this Tool

The Natura 2000 Tool combines GBIF species occurrence records with European habitat maps and conservation-relevant species lists to assess biodiversity within a Natura2000 site of interest. It supports Natura 2000 site management by providing an overview of recorded species, habitat types, and their associations.

### Features

- **Species Lists:** Identify species recorded within the study area, including species listed under the EU Birds and Habitats Directives and invasive alien species of Union concern.

- **EUNIS Habitat Maps:** Explore the spatial distribution, extent, and composition of EUNIS habitat types through interactive maps.

- **Annex I Habitats:** Identify potential correspondences between mapped EUNIS habitat types and habitats listed under Annex I of the EU Habitats Directive.

- **Characteristic Species:** Compare observed species with reference lists of characteristic species for each habitat type and identify which have been recorded within the corresponding habitat.

- **Data Quality and Monitoring Coverage:** Explore the temporal distribution of GBIF records, coordinate uncertainty, and spatial coverage of habitat classifications.

### Results

The tool produces an interactive dashboard with species inventories, habitat maps, habitat-area statistics, and species–habitat matching summaries.

Characteristic-species matching percentages indicate the proportion of listed species detected within each mapped habitat type, not ecological completeness. Results reflect available occurrence data and may be affected by sampling bias, incomplete detection, and uncertainty in habitat classifications.
