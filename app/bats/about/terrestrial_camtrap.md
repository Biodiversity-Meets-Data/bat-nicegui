### About this Tool

The Camera Trap Analysis Tool processes standardized camera-trap datasets in Camtrap DP format to summarize wildlife observations, species activity, and monitoring effort. It transforms camera-trap records into interactive visualizations that support biodiversity monitoring and conservation management.

### Features

- **Species List:** Explore wildlife species detected by camera traps, including species listed under the EU Birds and Habitats Directives.

- **Camera Trap Maps:** Visualize camera-trap locations and recorded wildlife observations on interactive maps.

- **Species Activity Patterns:** Examine daily activity patterns of detected species based on the timing of independent camera-trap records.

- **Monitoring Coverage:** Assess the spatial and temporal distribution of camera-trap monitoring, including annual monitoring coverage.

- **Monitoring Summary:** Review key statistics such as the number of camera sites, nominal camera-days, recorded species, and survey period.

### Results

The tool generates an interactive dashboard displaying detected species, monitoring locations, activity patterns, and sampling effort.

Species lists indicate which animals were recorded during monitoring, while activity curves describe when detections occurred throughout the day. Monitoring statistics help assess the extent and consistency of data collection.

Results are descriptive and do not directly estimate species abundance or population trends. Detection frequencies and activity patterns may be influenced by camera placement, monitoring effort, and species-specific detectability.
