### About this Tool

The Species Distribution Modeling Tool predicts potential habitat suitability under current and future climate conditions. It combines GBIF occurrence records with CHELSA bioclimatic variables and applies MaxEnt-based species distribution models to explore species–climate relationships and potential distribution changes.

### Features

- **Climate Suitability Predictions:** Generate interactive maps of predicted habitat suitability based on current climate conditions.

- **Future Climate Projections:** Explore potential species distributions under different climate-change scenarios and climate models.

- **Habitat Change Maps:** Visualize projected changes in habitat suitability, including potential gains and losses of suitable habitat.

- **Suitable Habitat Area:** Estimate and compare the extent of potentially suitable habitat across climate periods and scenarios.

- **Response Curves:** Examine how predicted habitat suitability changes along individual environmental gradients.

- **Variable Importance:** Identify the environmental predictors contributing most strongly to model predictions.

- **Model Evaluation and Uncertainty:** Assess predictive performance, variation among climate projections, and environmental extrapolation.

### Results

The tool produces an interactive dashboard with current and future suitability maps, habitat-change estimates, response curves, and model evaluation statistics.

Higher suitability values indicate climate conditions more similar to those associated with observed species occurrences, not necessarily species presence. Future projections describe potential climatic suitability changes rather than guaranteed distribution shifts.

Interpretation should consider model performance, sampling bias, climate-model uncertainty, and extrapolation beyond observed environmental conditions.
